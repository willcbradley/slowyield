Prestige

Society needs competent people.

We often idolise the lone genius neurosurgeon, and not without reason.

But as the landscape of interventions shifts and people scramble to fortify their scopes, it seems likely that the correlation between competence and prestige will start to break down. The faster science advances, the more this happens.

So, if our goal is to do great work, we'll probably need to look beyond existing status games.

What might we aim for instead?
