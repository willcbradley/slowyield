Frontiers

Hope requires growth.

It forces us to think from principles, and bet against sentiment, and figure out new, better solutions to the problems on the horizon.

But sentiment exists for a reason.

New ideas are fragile, and almost always fall flat. So growth tends to be concentrated in small pockets of smart, risk-tolerant people.

We call these areas "frontiers". They can exist in physical space (like a city) or in idea-space (like a field), but the premise is the same.

These frontiers seem rare in modern medicine. Where have they gone?
