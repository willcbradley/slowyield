Strength

The future is uncertain.

We're staring down the barrel of the biggest technological changes in history. Nothing is guaranteed, nowhere is safe, and nobody knows what happens next.

What should we rely on, in a world where we can't rely on anything?

Strength.

The strength to master the fundamentals, without falling prey to dogma.

The strength to ignore status games, without arrogantly discarding tradition.

The strength to resist demoralisation. To work on meaningful problems, regardless of the challenges, and despite the risk of failure. No matter what.

And what can we hope to get in return?
