Hope

Life is difficult.

Terrible things happen to great people. Everybody gets sick, and everybody dies. By all accounts, it looks like the game has been rigged against us.

What should we do, in the face of this?

Make things better.

But to do this, we need a definition of "better". We need to imagine a future that's exciting enough to justify today's pain. We need hope.

What does this mean, practically?
