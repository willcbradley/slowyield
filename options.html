Options

Reliance removes choice.

If we're dependent on a conventional medical career for survival, then we have no choice but to play by the rules, no matter how arbitrary they become.

The solution is to have options.

This lets us focus whole-heartedly on medicine, knowing we can move into other opportunities should it make sense.

But how to do this? And as tech improves, how will things change?
